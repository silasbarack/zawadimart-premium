**Status of this project**

  ZawadiMart site, all motion graphics, subtitles (Sheng/Kiswahili plus English), the disclosure,
  the WhatsApp ending and original background music.

  slots”) and re-render; nothing else needs to change.

## Live-action shot list and generation prompts

Shoot or generate each at **1080×1920, 30 fps, about 0.5 s longer than its slot** (for trimming),
with **synced dialogue audio inside the clip**. Keep each actor’s face, hair and clothes identical
across their shots (use the same reference image / seed / character ID).

Shared style for every prompt:
> Photorealistic vertical 9:16 handheld-but-steady footage, natural Nairobi daylight, 35 mm lens look,
> shallow depth of field, true-to-life medium-brown and dark skin tones, modern Kenyan urban
> smart-casual fashion, genuine relaxed expressions, natural hand anatomy, no on-screen text, no
> logos, phone and laptop screens angled away or softly out of focus (the real screen recordings are
> cut in separately).

## Voices and audio

- **Dialogue**: native Kenyan speakers (real voice actors, or a voice service with Kenyan
  English/Kiswahili voices), Nairobi accents, Sheng said naturally. Say the scene 2 line exactly as
  requested; only the subtitle spelling is tidied (“ku-design”).
- **Voice-over (scene 4)**: warm, confident Kenyan voice, Kiswahili/Sheng mix, 25.3–34.7 s
  (≈ 9.4 s). File: `public/audio/vo-scene4.wav`.
- **Music**: `public/audio/music.wav`, an original track generated by `tools/music.py` (no licence
  needed). It plays at about −20 dB under speech and comes up in the gaps.
- Target mix: dialogue peaks around −6 dBFS; final loudness about −14 LUFS for TikTok.
